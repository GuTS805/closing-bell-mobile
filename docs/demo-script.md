# Demo video script (about 2 minutes)

Record on the phone with Android's screen recorder, portrait, notifications silenced (Do Not Disturb), battery saver off. Speak the lines or add them as captions. Times are targets.

**Before recording:** install v0.2.0, clear app data once so the intro shows, place the widget on an empty home page, remove any personal apps from that page, and keep Solflare installed. Do not show the Solflare recovery phrase screen.

| Time | On screen | Say |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0:00–0:12 | Home screen with the Closing Bell widget | "This is what a Jupiter swap into tokenized stocks costs right now, over xStocks' own price. SPYx is red: over my limit." |
| 0:12–0:25 | Open the app; swipe through the 3-step intro | "Same token, different prices. The swap screen doesn't show the gap, and the slippage that would protect you is a guess." |
| 0:25–0:40 | Markets tab, scroll the four cards | "For $1,000, Jupiter's live price next to xStocks' price, and how far over it I'd pay." |
| 0:40–1:00 | Open SPYx, amount 1000, limit 1.00%, Check | "I set the most I'll pay over xStocks' price and check the live route for my exact amount." |
| 1:00–1:20 | Protect card, tap Copy, tap Open in Jupiter, show USDC → SPYx | "Within my limit, so it gives me two settings. With this minimum received, if the price runs past my limit before the swap lands, Jupiter's on-chain check cancels it. One tap opens the swap." |
| 1:20–1:35 | Back; set limit 0.10%, Check, tap Alert me | "Over my limit? I don't guess. The app checks in the background and tells me when it's back within." |
| 1:35–1:45 | Scroll to the history chart | "Every check builds a week of history on the phone." |
| 1:45–1:55 | Wallet tab, Connect with Solflare, then Disconnect | "Read-only Mobile Wallet Adapter: it shows my xStocks and never asks me to sign." |
| 1:55–2:05 | Share this check, show the share sheet | "And I can share the check." |
| 2:05–2:10 | Back to the widget | "Closing Bell: know the cost, set the limit, let the chain enforce it." |

Keep one honest line on screen or in the caption: "Compares Jupiter's price with xStocks' indicative token price, not the underlying share. Never signs or moves funds."
